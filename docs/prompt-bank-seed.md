# Prompt Bank Seed (v0 static prompts)

The initial static bank behind System 1's `prompt-source` interface (see `prd-core-messaging-loop.md` F2). Bar: answerable honestly in 15–30 seconds, light, zero emotional risk, fun to see your partner's answer to. Dispatch order should be shuffled, with no-repeat tracking.

1. What's your favorite thing to cook?
2. Best movie you've seen in the last 6 months?
3. What song have you had on repeat lately?
4. What's your ideal breakfast?
5. How old were you when you learned to ride a bike?
6. What's a smell that instantly takes you back somewhere?
7. What's your dream coffee/drink order if money and calories didn't matter?
8. What's the last thing that made you laugh out loud?
9. What's your go-to comfort show?
10. If you could teleport anywhere just for dinner tonight, where?
11. What's a small purchase (under $25) that genuinely improved your life?
12. What were you obsessed with at age 10?
13. What's your most-used emoji, and why that one?
14. Window or aisle seat, and what's the reasoning?
15. What's one food you flat-out refuse to eat?
16. Best concert or live event you've ever been to?
17. What did 8-year-old you want to be when you grew up?
18. What's your favorite time of day, and what makes it yours?
19. Which app do you open first in the morning?
20. What's a skill you'd download Matrix-style right now if you could?
21. What was your favorite class you ever took in school?
22. Best meal you've had this month?
23. Free Saturday, zero obligations: what does it look like?
24. What's a tiny thing that always makes your day a little better?
25. Who was your first celebrity crush?
26. What's your favorite family tradition?
27. What game (board, video, card, anything) could you play forever?
28. What's something you've been meaning to try but still haven't?
29. Best gift you've ever received?
30. What's the first concert/movie/book you remember loving as a kid?
